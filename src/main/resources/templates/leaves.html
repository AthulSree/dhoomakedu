Leave Portal Guide

  • 1. Select Employee Choose the correct personnel from the dropdown list.
  • 2. Absent Dates Enter just the days (e.g. 2, 13) if 2nd and 13th was absent. Comma-separated values only. FYI: Focus on this field and click Enter/Tab/Space to see what Shammy says!
  • 3. Half-Day Leaves (Day/2) Use this to mention half-day leaves. Add half-day counts only for those who have the AKAL work order. Should be entered only if strictly necessary (Nannaayitt alochichitt mathram).
  • 4. Used Combo Leaves Mention all the leaves, including combo leaves, as comma-separated values. The combo will be applied to it directly.
  • 5. Upload Attendance (Optional) Uploading the PDF is optional. If uploaded, the system will automatically print available, used, and pending combo leaves on the document.
  • 6. MPR Ownership: It's Your Responsibility Batch printing, submission to officers, signatures, scanning, and portal uploads are handled by colleague volunteers as a courtesy facilitation to help the team, not an assigned job duty.
    • Each employee is strictly responsible for submitting their leave numbers on time before the cutoff.
    • Unsubmitted leaves cannot be processed with the monthly batch; the team will not send personal reminders.
    • Signed MPRs are uploaded to the portal. Employees must download/collect their own documents; no personal delivery or dispatch will be made.
